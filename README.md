# Proofs of Nath–Fasfous–Das–Shang's commuting and surface conjectures, and a sharp form of Tak–Dutta–Nath's clique theorem

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

For Nath–Fasfous–Das–Shang's Conjectures 1–3 (Symmetry 13 (2021), 1651, Section 4, p. 11), we prove the surface and commuting-graph statements and give an alternative proof with exact equality cases for Tak–Dutta–Nath's prior clique result (arXiv:2605.22160v1 (2026), Theorem 5.4, pp. 14–15). Every planar or toroidal graph is non-CN-hyperenergetic. For every finite nonabelian group, its noncentral commuting graph satisfies E_CN ≤ n(n-z-2) < 2(n-1)(n-2), where n counts noncentral elements and z counts central elements; the same bound holds for every finite noncommutative ring, without an identity assumption. The group argument combines the published estimate E_CN ≤ 4m with a proper-centralizer count. A neighborhood-clique decomposition proves the surface statement. For a union of cliques, equality with the complete-graph benchmark occurs exactly for one clique or two isolated vertices.
